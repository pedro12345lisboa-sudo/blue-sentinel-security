Exfiltration is confirmed by volume, destination, and time — not by intent. This playbook organizes the reading of those three and keeps the chain of custody while the channel is closed.

<Callout type="warning" title="Defensive boundary">Triage uses network flow, proxy logs, and authentication records. No step involves recovering exposed content on your own or interacting with the malicious destination.</Callout>

## Context

`bs-net-suspicious-outbound` covers an outbound connection to an unusual port or destination from a Windows workstation, and `bs-cloud-login-new-country` flags cloud access from a location never seen for that account. The combination of the two — anomalous outbound traffic and an identity in a new place — is the pattern that most quickly separates legitimate synchronization from exfiltration.

## When not to use

An approved migration or backup window, with compatible volume and a known destination, closes with the exception documented. This playbook applies when there is no matching change or when the destination is not part of the approved services inventory.
