# Short story

I started studying security from the networking and systems side and arrived at Blue Team through a practical question: how do you know something strange is happening before it is too late?

The answer became a routine — write detection, test it against real logs, tune the alert, document why the rule exists. This portfolio is the record of that: every project has a problem, context, solution, technical decision and measured result. Anything not yet measured shows up as a placeholder, never as an invented number.
